OoT3D Model Importer

Turn ModLoader64 Ocarina of Time player models (.pak / .zobj) and voice packs into an Ocarina of Time 3D mod for Citra or a modded 3DS.

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded, and no game files are hosted here: you provide them from your own copy of OoT3D.

1 Mod files

ML64 .pak files, .zip files or loose .zobj files. Add an adult model, a child model, equipment packs and/or a voice pack. MM models inside a pack are skipped.

    2 Your OoT3D game files

    From your own extracted romfs (ExtractedRomFS). Pick the files listed below, or pick the whole romfs folder.

    • Add mod files first to see which game files are needed.

    Good to know
    
    
      
      
    • Results vary from model to model: some held items may sit slightly differently than on the N64, and the first-person slingshot can show a small stray sliver.
      • 
      
    • Tunic colours (Kokiri / Goron / Zora) work for adult models. Turn off the OoT3D Randomizer's Custom Tunic Colors option, which is not compatible with imported models. Child models stay Kokiri green, as in the base game.
      • 
      
    • A voice pack replaces Link's voice clips; any of Link's voice clips the pack doesn't cover are silenced.
